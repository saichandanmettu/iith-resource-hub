About

Welcome to IIT-H Resource Hub.

A platform designed with the sole purpose of providing valuable academic resources to students of IIT Hyderabad. Developed by students, for students, it offers a centralised repository of notes, assignments, quizzes, and other academic materials — an inclusive space where knowledge can be shared and accessed freely, helping students excel in their academic journeys.
