Do send

4 items
  • Course code. Helps file and index everything instantly.

  • Clear scan. Flat phone photos in good lighting are fine.
  • Semester batches. Sharing a batch zip helps everyone.

Please don’t

4 items
  • Live assignments. Don’t post ongoing or active coursework.
  • Pirated books. Send titles — Bookshelf links legal copies.
  • Confidential notes. Avoid restricted instructor handouts.
  • Personal files. Don’t post private documents or grades.
