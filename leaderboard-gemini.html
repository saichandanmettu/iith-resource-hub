How Points Are Earned

Every file you upload is reviewed and scored based on preparation effort and scarcity for IITH students.

+10pts
Past Exam Papers & Quizzes Mid-sem, end-sem, and quiz question papers with or without solutions.
+8pts
Assignments & Lab Sheets Past assignment problems, verified lab manuals, and tutorial sheets.
+5pts
Lecture Notes & Slide Decks Comprehensive student lecture notes, summaries, and professor slide sets.
+2pts
Prescribed Reference Books Prescribed course textbook suggestions with author and edition cataloging.
Points are live-derived from verified files in the archive. Opting for anonymous credit archives your file without listing on the leaderboard.
