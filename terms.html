A few things to know Before you use the archive

A student-built archive for finding, sharing, and passing on course material at IITH.

Abhyas Last updated Aug 2026
  1. 01

    Not official

    No connection to IIT Hyderabad’s administration or its official portals. Nobody from the institute reviews what goes up here.

  2. 02

    Study aid, not a shortcut

    Use it to revise and to check your own understanding. Submitting any of it as your work is misconduct.

  3. 03

    Expect gaps

    Nothing here is verified. Where a grade depends on it, check against what your instructor gave you.

  4. 04

    Tell us

    Corrections, missing papers, and takedown requests go to one inbox.

    Email us

Questions worth asking

  • Use Share a file in the header and send us the course code, resource type, and file. We’ll review it before it joins the archive.

  • Yes. Send the course code and the resource you’re looking for to the address in Tell us. We’ll add it to the list and fill it when a copy turns up.

  • Anyone from IITH can share a useful file. Please upload only material you’re allowed to share, and include the course code so others can find it.
