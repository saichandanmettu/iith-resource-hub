A few things to know As you get started

Abhyas terms of use Last updated Aug 2026
01

No official affiliation

Abhyas is run and managed by students for students. There is no direct connection with the administration or course instructors.

02

Study aid, not a shortcut

Use it to revise and to check your own understanding. Submitting any of it as your work is misconduct.

03

Use sensibly

Resources uploaded here are shared by students without official verification. Use them to practice, but don’t assume past questions or patterns will repeat.

04

Tell us

Corrections, missing papers, and takedown requests go to one inbox.
