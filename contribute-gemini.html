The 4 Principles of a Good File

What makes the archive great

  • Legible, high-contrast scans: Clear phone captures with all 4 corners in frame and no shadow occluding questions.
  • Accurate course codes & year: CS2110 is distinct from CS1010; label whether it was 2024 or 2025.
  • Entire semester bundles: A zip with Mid-Sem + End-Sem + Quizzes is 10x more valuable than a single page.
  • Clean handwritten notes: High quality structured summary notes that save someone's exam night.

What gets rejected

  • Live or ongoing assignment solutions: Material for coursework that is currently open turns sharing into cheating.
  • Confidential or non-disclosed exam sheets: Material explicitly restricted by instructors from circulation.
  • Pirated commercial book PDFs: Send the prescribed title and we will link to library references instead.
  • Blurry, unreadable snapshots: Cropped, out-of-focus or unreadable camera dumps.
